Notes are written on the **staff**: five horizontal lines, counted from the bottom up. A note sits
either on a line, with the line through its head, or in a **space** between two lines.

The **treble clef**, also called the G clef, curls around the second line and gives it the note
:note[G4]. Every other note is counted from there.

```staff The note on the second line, the one the treble clef curls around
4/4 G4/whole
```

In the treble clef the lines, from the bottom up, are :note[E4], :note[G4], :note[B4], :note[D5]
and :note[F5]. The spaces between them are :note[F4], :note[A4], :note[C5] and :note[E5].

```staff The notes on the five lines, from the bottom up
4/4 E4/quarter G4/quarter B4/quarter D5/quarter F5/whole
```

```staff The notes in the four spaces, from the bottom up
4/4 F4/quarter A4/quarter C5/quarter E5/quarter
```

So the notes follow each other step by step: line, space, line. One step up from a line is the
space above it, and one step up from a space is the line above it.

Notes above and below the staff are written on short extra lines, the **ledger lines**, and
between them. Below the staff, :note[D4] hangs under the first line, middle :note[C4] sits on the
first ledger line, :note[B3] hangs under it and :note[A3] sits on the second. Above the staff,
:note[G5] sits on top of the fifth line, :note[A5] on the first ledger line, :note[B5] above it and
:note[C6] on the second.

```staff Down from the staff: under the first line, on the first ledger line, under it, on the second
4/4 D4/quarter C4/quarter B3/quarter A3/quarter
```

```staff Up from the staff: over the fifth line, on the first ledger line, over it, on the second
4/4 G5/quarter A5/quarter B5/quarter C6/quarter
```
